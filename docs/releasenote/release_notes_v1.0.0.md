# SonderShield 1.0.0

The first release. An ad, tracker and malware blocker for Android: no account, and nothing leaves your phone.

## Blocking
- Blocks ads, trackers and malware in every app through a local DNS-only VPN. No root needed.
- Block lists: StevenBlack, AdGuard DNS, URLhaus, HaGeZi, OISD, EasyList, uBlock Origin filters, plus regional lists switched on for your language. Add your own lists by URL.
- Your own block and allow rules, and a tester to see why a domain is blocked.
- Encrypted DNS (DNS over HTTPS) with Cloudflare, Quad9, Google, AdGuard, or your own server.
- Automatic list updates on Wi-Fi.

## HTTPS filtering
- Hides ad spaces and anti-adblock walls inside pages in the browsers you pick.
- Uses a certificate made on your phone; the app walks you through installing it and checks it.
- Banking, payment and government sites are never touched, nor any site whose app refuses the certificate.

## Tunnel
- Full tunnel mode: all traffic goes through SonderShield.
- WireGuard: import a .conf file and your traffic leaves through your own server or VPN provider, still filtered.
- Root mode: on rooted phones, blocks without a VPN so another VPN can run alongside.
- Apps you choose can bypass SonderShield.

## And
- Live log showing which app asked, with search and filters; allow or block from the log.
- Home screen stats, Quick Settings tile (long press opens the app) and a home screen widget.
- Starts on boot, works as an always-on VPN.
- Settings backup and restore, log export.
- Themes: Default (pure black), Light, System, Material You, with your own accent colour.
- English, French and German.
