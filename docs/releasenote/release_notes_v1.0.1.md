# SonderShield 1.0.1

## New
- **Filter tab** in place of Log: your block lists grouped into Ad blocking, Privacy, Security, Regional and Custom, with search, rule counts, last update and source for each. The log now lives inside Filter.
- **Firewall**: in Apps, switch to *No internet* to cut an app off completely, on Wi-Fi and mobile data. Needs Full tunnel or Root mode.
- **More lists**: AdGuard Base, AdGuard Mobile Ads, AdGuard Tracking Protection, AdGuard Social Media, Malware Domain List, Phishing Army, and ABPVN for Vietnamese.

## Changed
- List updates and the auto-update setting moved from Settings to the Filter tab; Settings is shorter.

Update over 1.0.0: your settings and lists are kept.
