# SonderShield 2.5.0

## Android
- **Protection profiles:** not sure which lists to pick? Choose **Default**, **Strict**, **Family**, **Gaming** or **Strict Family**, or make your own. Find them in Filter › Profiles, or tap the new profile button at the top of Home.
- **Schedules:** switch a profile on at set times, like Family on weekday evenings. Long-press a profile for Schedule, and for your own ones Edit and Delete.
- **New lists:** HaGeZi NSFW and HaGeZi Gambling, in a new Family safety group.
- **Cleaner Home:** one short line under the headline.
- Long-pressing the Quick Settings tile now opens Home.
- A small secret is hidden somewhere in the app.

## Windows
- **Protection profiles and schedules,** the same as on the phone: in Filter › Profiles and from the new profile button on Home. Right-click a profile for Schedule, Edit and Delete.
- **New lists:** HaGeZi NSFW and HaGeZi Gambling.
- The About section shows the SonderShield mark. It may be worth a few clicks.

Updating on Windows: Settings › Updates.
